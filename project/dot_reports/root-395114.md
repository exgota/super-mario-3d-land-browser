## Recovered behavior

Conversions d/i/u/o/x/X/p, c, s, n and plain %% are supported. Decimal fields or `*` supply width and precision. Negative star width sets left alignment; negative star precision becomes unspecified. h/hh narrow integer arguments, ll consumes 8-byte-aligned64-bit arguments, and l retains32-bit behavior. Strings use precision as a maximum length. Output tracks the full logical count after capacity exhaustion, truncates writes, and terminates at the cursor when space remains or capacity-1 otherwise.
