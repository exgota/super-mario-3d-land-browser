Each invocation records and compares 95 semantic call events, including all formatted strings, promoted double argument bits, integer arguments, receiver offsets/capacities, color inputs and copied label bytes. Stack-local scratch contents and pointer values of literal formats are intentionally not compared as observable outputs.

Fixtures cover filled and pseudorandom initial storage, six aligned receiver locations including page crossings, zero/odd/self/interior/max-word opaque owner pointers, all four floating rounding modes plus FZ/DN combinations, and repeated initialization. This is a bounded constructor test, not a general printf proof or gameplay claim.
