## Algorithm and original boundaries

Back-facing contacts are excluded by key without producing a hit. Other contacts use the original kind and side/vertex getters. Triangle contacts first compute the swept contact point projected into the contact plane. A point inside all three side planes uses its plane distance directly. An outside point selects a neighboring vertex and side, intersects the projected direction with that boundary, and solves the corresponding sphere quadratic. Non-triangle contacts use their supplied contact position in the same quadratic.

The quadratic keeps the original stable-root selection: calculate a signed numerator using the discriminant square root, divide by the direction's squared length, and use the other root when the selected root is negative. The stored fraction is the current sample fraction minus this result. Each emitted surface is copied through +0x5B; the contact's later extension is not part of the output surface.

Keys are appended to the bounded ring, including rejected back-facing contacts; a full ring drops further append operations. Output is sorted ascending by its first float through original comparator `001676B0`, original partition/sort helper `0039EC9C` with count as its third argument, original insertion helper `0039E180`, and an inlined insertion tail after the first 16 records.
